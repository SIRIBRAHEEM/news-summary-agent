Wednesday, 11 November 2026

Iran said the Strait of Hormuz would stay shut until the US met its seven conditions for peace talks, security chief Mohsen Rezaei told Al Mayadeen TV, denying any negotiations were under way. Iran's Revolutionary Guards said three oil tankers caught fire on Tuesday after hitting mines, while the UK maritime agency reported two tanker incidents. In Paris, US Secretary of State Marco Rubio said the economic campaign was "so devastating that right now there is no need for major combat operations" (Al Jazeera).

Russia and Ukraine agreed a large prisoner-of-war exchange as two days of US-mediated talks ended in Abu Dhabi, with no breakthrough on ending the war. US envoy Jared Kushner called it the "biggest humanitarian release of prisoners", with numbers to be finalised on Thursday; he and Steve Witkoff will next meet Vladimir Putin in Moscow, then Volodymyr Zelenskyy in Dublin (AP).

The Democratic Republic of Congo confirmed 11,042 Ebola cases and 5,387 deaths, its government said on Tuesday, in the second-largest outbreak on record.

In Washington, the House voted 211-204 to send an impeachment resolution against Defence Secretary Pete Hegseth to committee, effectively killing it, and Republicans chose Jim Jordan as their new House leader. The Justice Department charged three Iran-linked operatives over alleged plots in the US and Europe, and Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent moved to a military base after threats.

At the COP31 climate summit in Antalya, UN chief Antonio Guterres warned leaders the slow response had put the world "on a path to destruction". Israel swore in a new parliament with no government formed, after an election that cost Benjamin Netanyahu his majority, as Israeli strikes killed at least seven people in Gaza. Nvidia shares fell 5.4% to $169.04 on Tuesday, after falling for a second session.
